## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a citizen-centered, compassionate digital portal for public social services. It strips away bureaucratic friction in favor of clarity, human warmth, and institutional integrity. The visual philosophy fuses **Modern Corporate Civics** with **Humanist Minimalism**—balancing the authority of a municipal department with the empathetic, approachable posture required for social assistance.

### Target Audience & Voice
The portal serves citizens across diverse socioeconomic backgrounds, literacy levels, and technical capabilities, alongside field social workers and regional administrators. The design voice is:
- **Reassuring & Steadfast:** Eliminates anxiety through explicit labels, predictable flows, and calm structural balance.
- **Empathetic & Accessible:** Non-judgmental tone, ultra-clear visual signposting, and high legibility.
- **Modern & Transparent:** Demonstrates accountable, prompt public service without cold corporate detachment.

### Visual Style
Clean layouts feature soft off-white canvas foundations, structured content cards, gentle ambient elevations, and intentional amber accents to direct user focus toward high-value social services, status tracking, and assistance submissions.

## Colors

The color palette centers on public trust, visual accessibility, and clear focal hierarchy, adhering strictly to WCAG AA contrast guidelines across all interface tiers.

### Roles & Semantic Mapping
- **Primary (`#0F5C7A` - Civic Teal):** Represents governance, dependability, and calm reassurance. Used for navigation bars, primary headings, key icon containers, and interactive anchors.
- **Secondary (`#F5A623` - Warm Amber):** Delivers warmth and urgency without alarmism. Dedicated to primary conversion actions, high-priority submission buttons, emergency helpline links, and active state highlights. Text on amber surfaces must utilize deep ink (`#1E293B`) to maintain WCAG AAA compliance.
- **Tertiary (`#2E8B57` - Social Green):** Conveys resolution, verified status, social aid distribution confirmation, and positive citizen eligibility notices.
- **Neutral (`#5A6E75` - Slate Sea):** Calibrated for secondary information, metadata, table borders, and structural dividers.

### Surface Tones & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base:** Soft off-white (`#F7FAFB`) prevents harsh eye fatigue common with pure `#FFFFFF` during prolonged document reading.
- **Surface Elevation:** True white (`#FFFFFF`) for cards and interactive inputs, bordered by subtle neutral tints (`#E2E8F0`) to establish clean contrast planes.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Charcoal Slate (`#102229`) for headings and body content, providing crisp contrast against light surfaces.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent, friendly headlines with **Inter** for sustained readability in detailed civic program guides, legal terms, and verification forms.

### Usage Guidance
- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its rounded terminals and open apertures offer an encouraging, modern atmosphere, counteracting systemic administrative rigidity.
- **Body & Forms:** Set in Inter. Chosen for tall x-height, neutral letterforms, and distinct numerical glyphs, preventing errors during the input and reading of ID cards (NIK), family registration codes, and assistance fund figures.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Always maintain one dominant headline level per view. Never compress line heights below 1.3x for body copy to preserve comfort for older citizens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances dense informational utility with generous breathing room to reduce cognitive overwhelm.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width constrained to `1280px` for optimal eye travel. Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer page margins to `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, and `1rem` (`16px`) screen margins. Form sections collapse into single vertical stacks.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing follows a strict 4px/8px base rhythm. Content modules inside service cards use `space-md` (`16px`) to group connected elements, while parent sections separate via `space-xl` (`32px`) to `space-2xl` (`48px`) to allow users to visually process discrete steps in application pipelines.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **ambient shadows** paired with soft structural borders, maintaining clarity without harsh skeuomorphism.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Default canvas (`#F7FAFB`). Used for page backgrounds and disabled container areas.
- **Level 1 (Resting Card / Container):** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border (`#E4ECEF`), shadow `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 92, 122, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Imparts a gentle lift with a subtle teal tint.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Dropdown):** Background `#FFFFFF`, shadow `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(15, 92, 122, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Signals responsiveness when hovering over citizen service modules.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Persistent Banner / Toast):** Shadow `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 92, 122, 0.18), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`. Reserved for identity verification modals and urgent public service alerts.

## Shapes

The system uses a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard, creating an approachable, secure appearance.

### Application Tokens
- **Standard Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Input fields, inline filter chips, table rows, and secondary action triggers.
- **Cards & Banners (`1rem` / `16px` - `rounded-lg`):** Main service cards, status summary blocks, document upload dropzones, and alert banners.
- **Modals & Hero Blocks (`1.5rem` / `24px` - `rounded-xl`):** Onboarding dialogs, application summary sheets, and prominent hero showcase banners.
- **Full Radius (`9999px`):** Status badges, circular profile avatars, and icon pill badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid warm amber (`#F5A623`), dark ink text (`#1A202C`), font weight 600, radius `8px` (`0.5rem`), padding `12px 24px`. Subtle hover state deepens to `#E09215`. Used for primary actions like "Ajukan Bantuan" (Apply for Aid) or "Kirim Berkas" (Submit Documents).
- **Secondary (Institutional):** Deep teal (`#0F5C7A`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), radius `8px`. Used for navigation, portal authentication, and primary confirmation steps.
- **Outlined / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#0F5C7A`, text `#0F5C7A`. Used for secondary actions such as "Cek Status" (Check Status) or "Batal" (Cancel).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#D1DEE2`, radius `8px`, typography `body-md`. Focus state switches border to `#0F5C7A` with a 3px outer glow `rgba(15, 92, 122, 0.15)`.
- **Validation:** Clear error text with warning icon in `#D9383A`. Always accompany color states with descriptive instructional text.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Teal fill (`#0F5C7A`) when selected, minimum touch target 44px on mobile devices.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), subtle border `#E4ECEF`, level 1 elevation.
- Service cards include a rounded icon tile (teal or amber background), title (`headline-sm`), concise 2-line summary, and an explicit arrow link indicator.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Pending/Proses:** Soft amber tint (`#FEF3C7`), text `#92400E`, radius full pill.
- **Approved/Disetujui:** Soft mint tint (`#D1FAE5`), text `#065F46`, radius full pill.
- **Rejected/Ditolak:** Soft rose tint (`#FEE2E2`), text `#991B1B`, radius full pill.

### Citizen-Specific Modules
- **NIK Validation Input:** Formatted with grouped digit spacing (4-4-4-4) to reduce transcription errors.
- **Step Process Tracker:** Horizontal progress bar with numbered teal circles, connecting paths, and current-step status announcements for screen readers.
